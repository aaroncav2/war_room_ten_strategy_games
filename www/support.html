Common questions

How do I play?

Pick one of the ten wars, choose a side, and select three actions each period. Every action shows its chance of success. After you execute the period, historical events and the other side's moves play out, and the bars update. Aim for the goals shown for your side.

Where is my progress saved?

Stars and ranks are stored on your device only. Deleting the app erases them.

Why do I see ads?

The app is free and shows a banner ad at the bottom, provided by Google AdMob. See the Privacy Policy for details and for how to change your tracking choice.

There is no sound.

Check that the Sound button in the app is on, that your iPhone isn't in silent mode (the ring/silent switch or Control Center), and that the volume is up.

A game won't load or looks wrong.

Close the app completely and reopen it. If it keeps happening, email us the details above.

War Room uses simplified models of contested history. They cannot capture the human cost of war.
